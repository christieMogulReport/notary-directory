Need something notarized in Washington, DC after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are DC places that post notary hours on evenings and weekends, plus mobile notaries who come to your home, office or hospital room.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** The UPS Store's own pages say notaries are scheduled based on staff availability and recommend calling ahead or making an appointment. Holiday hours change too: all 20 stores below list Thanksgiving, November 26, as closed (November 27 is listed as regular hours). Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 22, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** 5 of the 20 DC UPS Stores listed below post Sunday notary hours: Navy Yard (1100 New Jersey Ave, 12–5 PM), 14th & Monroe St NW (1380 Monroe St NW, 10 AM–4 PM), H Street NE (921 H St NE, 11 AM–4 PM), 18th St NW (2202 18th St NW, 10 AM–3 PM) and GWU Campus (825 21st St NW, 10 AM–3 PM). Navy Yard posts the latest Sunday notary hours, until 5 PM.
- **Weekday evenings:** Seven stores below post notary hours until 7 PM on weekdays: Navy Yard, 14th & Monroe St NW, H Street NE, Near White House (1030 15th St NW), 7th Street NW (1835 7th St NW), Dakota Crossing (2490 Market St NE) and The Wharf (996 Maine Ave SW). DC Mobile Notary posts mobile service hours until 9 PM Monday through Thursday.
- **Saturday:** All 20 stores below post Saturday notary hours. 14th & Monroe St NW (9 AM–6 PM) and The Wharf (10 AM–6 PM) post the latest.
- **At home, at a hospital, or later than the stores:** DC Mobile Notary posts Saturday and Sunday mobile hours of 10 AM–4 PM, and 202 Notary LLC advertises 24/7 mobile service by appointment.

A UPS Store's notary hours can be shorter than its store hours. Several DC stores are open on Sunday but do not post Sunday notary hours, so each listing shows the notary hours the store posts. One DC UPS Store was left out because it posts weekday notary hours only (9 AM–5 PM, no weekend hours): 1300 Pennsylvania Ave NW, inside the Ronald Reagan Building.

## When nothing is open: online notarization

DC law allows a notary public located in the District to notarize for a remotely located signer by audio-video technology (D.C. Code § 1–1231.13a: <https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/1-1231.13a>). DC notaries need a separate remote notary endorsement from the DC Office of Notary Commissions and Authentications to do this (<https://os.dc.gov/service/notary-commissions>). The national services below work by video call on your own device and generally use notaries commissioned in other states. Many documents are accepted that way, but some offices, courts, and foreign embassies may not accept an online notarization. Check with whoever will receive your document first.

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Find or check a DC notary

The DC Office of the Secretary runs a "Find a DC Notary Public Near Me" map that searches by address or ZIP code (<https://os.dc.gov/service/search-notary-public>). The office says the map does not include every DC notary, not all listed locations serve the public, and you should call or email the notary first. To verify a DC notary's commission, the same page links to an online verification request. The Office of Notary Commissions and Authentications does not notarize documents itself; it suggests checking with your bank, since many banks notarize for their customers.
