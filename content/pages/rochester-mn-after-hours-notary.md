Need something notarized in Rochester after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are Rochester places that post notary hours on evenings and weekends, plus mobile and online options for when everything is closed.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** The UPS Store's own pages say notaries are scheduled based on staff availability and recommend calling ahead. Holiday hours change too: both Rochester UPS Stores list Thanksgiving, November 26, as closed (November 27 is listed as regular hours), Christmas Eve, December 24, as a short day ending at 3 PM, and December 25, December 31 and January 1 as closed. Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 25, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** Both Rochester UPS Stores are closed on Sunday. Med City Ship (102 17th Ave NW) lists Sunday hours of 8 AM–1 PM on its own website, but its Chamber of Commerce listing says Sunday is by appointment, so call (507) 206-0203 first. Otherwise, use a mobile notary or an online notary (below).
- **Weekday evenings:** Med City Ship and The UPS Store Eastwood Plaza (1529 Hwy 14 E) both post hours until 7 PM on weekdays. The UPS Store at Maple Wood Square (3936-E Frontage Rd Hwy 52 N) posts notary hours until 6:30 PM.
- **Saturday:** The UPS Store Eastwood Plaza posts Saturday notary hours of 9 AM–5 PM, Maple Wood Square 9 AM–4 PM, and Med City Ship 8 AM–1 PM.
- **At home, a hotel or a hospital, or late at night:** Med City Ship offers on-demand mobile visits with posted fees, and 1MobileNotary says it books evening, weekend and holiday appointments in Rochester.
- **At Mayo Clinic on a weekday:** Mayo's free Concierge Services can notarize documents during weekday hours.

## FedEx Office in Rochester

FedEx Office's Rochester location page says documents are notarized online on your own device through FedEx Online Notary with Notarize, not by an in-store notary. ## When nothing is open: online notarization

Minnesota law (Minn. Stat. § 358.645) lets Minnesota notaries registered with the Secretary of State perform remote online notarizations over audio-video technology; the notary must be physically in Minnesota during the session (<https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/2026/cite/358.645>, <https://www.sos.mn.gov/notary-apostille/notary-help/remote-online-notarization-authorization-faq/>). The national services below work by video call on your own device and generally use notaries commissioned in other states. Many documents are accepted that way, but some offices, courts, and foreign embassies may not accept an online notarization. Check with whoever will receive your document first.

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Find or check a Minnesota notary

The Minnesota Secretary of State's Find A Notary tool lets you verify a notary's commission by name, commission number or business name, filtered by county; a notary's details page also shows whether they are authorized for remote online notarization (<https://notary.sos.mn.gov/search/searchfornotary>, <https://www.sos.mn.gov/notary-apostille/notary-help/verify-remote-online-notarization-authorization/>). Olmsted County Vital Records and Licensing, at the Government Center (151 4th Street SE), also notarizes signatures for $5 each, walk-in or by appointment, but says it cannot notarize deeds, mortgage documents, wills, or I-9 forms (<https://www.olmstedcounty.gov/residents/licensing-permits-records/notary>). If you have an account at a bank or credit union, ask whether a notary is on staff; branch notaries usually work weekday hours only.
