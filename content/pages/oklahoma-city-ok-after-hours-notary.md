Need something notarized in Oklahoma City after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are OKC places that post notary service with evening or weekend hours, plus mobile notaries who come to you.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** Notaries are often scheduled by staff availability, and holiday hours change. Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 19, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** Five OKC UPS Stores post Sunday notary hours. The UPS Store at 50th & N. May posts the longest: 12–5 PM. MidFirst Bank at Grand & Western (5800 N Western) and 74th & Western (7500 S Western) post Sunday lobby hours of 12–4 PM and list notary service. Edgemere Notary Service (mobile) posts Sunday hours of 11 AM–6 PM.
- **Weekday evenings:** The two MidFirst branches above post lobby hours until 8 PM on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (Wednesday until 5 PM). The UPS Store at 50th & N. May posts notary hours until 7 PM on weekdays. Edgemere Notary Service posts weekday hours of 6–8 PM.
- **Saturday:** All seven OKC UPS Stores and all five MidFirst branches below post Saturday hours.
- **Late night or at home:** The mobile notaries below advertise evening and weekend appointments. M&M's Notary lists a direct line for calls or texts 24/7.

A UPS Store's notary hours can be shorter than its store hours. Each listing shows the notary hours the store posts.

## FedEx Office in Oklahoma City

FedEx Office's Oklahoma City location pages say documents are notarized online on your own device through FedEx Online Notary with Notarize. The store can help you scan and print before and after the session. Source: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ok/oklahoma-city/office-4202> (all Oklahoma City FedEx locations: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ok/oklahoma-city>)

## When nothing is open: online notarization

Oklahoma has allowed remote online notarization since January 1, 2020 (Oklahoma Secretary of State: <https://oklahoma.gov/sos/notary/remote-online-notary-information.html>). Some documents or receiving offices may not accept an online notarization, so check first. These services work by video call on your own device:

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Check a notary's commission

You can look up an Oklahoma notary commission with the Oklahoma Secretary of State notary search: <https://business.sos.ok.gov/notary/search.aspx>. General notary information: <https://oklahoma.gov/sos/notary.html>
