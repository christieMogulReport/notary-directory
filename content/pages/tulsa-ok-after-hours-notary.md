Need something notarized in Tulsa after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are Tulsa places that post notary service with evening or weekend hours, plus mobile notaries who come to you.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** Notaries are often scheduled by staff availability, and holiday hours change. Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 18, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** The UPS Store on S Utica, N Peoria Ave, and S Olympia Ave post notary hours of 10 AM–3 PM on Sundays. MidFirst Bank at 71st & Memorial posts Sunday lobby hours of 12–4 PM and lists notary service.
- **Weekday evenings:** MidFirst Bank at 71st & Memorial posts lobby hours until 8 PM Monday–Friday. Several UPS Stores post notary hours until 6 or 6:30 PM.
- **Saturday:** All nine Tulsa UPS Stores and four MidFirst branches below post Saturday hours.
- **Late night or at home:** The mobile notaries below advertise evening and weekend appointments. Just Legal Solutions says it is available 24/7.

A UPS Store's notary hours can be shorter than its store hours. Each listing shows the notary hours the store posts.

## FedEx Office in Tulsa

FedEx Office's Tulsa location pages say documents are notarized online on your own device through FedEx Online Notary with Notarize. The store can help you scan and print before and after the session. Source: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ok/tulsa>

## When nothing is open: online notarization

Oklahoma has allowed remote online notarization since January 1, 2020 (Oklahoma Secretary of State: <https://oklahoma.gov/sos/notary/remote-online-notary-information.html>). Some documents or receiving offices may not accept an online notarization, so check first. These services work by video call on your own device:

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Check a notary's commission

You can look up an Oklahoma notary commission with the Oklahoma Secretary of State notary search: <https://business.sos.ok.gov/notary/search.aspx>. General notary information: <https://oklahoma.gov/sos/notary.html>
