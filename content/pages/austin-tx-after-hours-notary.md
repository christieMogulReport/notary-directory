Need something notarized in Austin after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Austin is easier than most cities: most of its UPS Stores post Sunday hours. Below are the Austin places with the latest posted evening and weekend notary hours, plus an evening-only appointment office, the Central Library, and mobile and online options for when everything is closed.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** The UPS Store's own pages say notaries are scheduled based on staff availability and recommend calling ahead. Holiday hours change too: every Austin UPS Store we checked lists Thanksgiving, November 26, as closed (November 27 is listed as regular hours), Christmas Eve, December 24, as a short day ending at 3 PM, and Christmas Day, December 25, as closed. The Austin Public Library is closed November 26 and 27. Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 26, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** The UPS Store Wells Branch Plaza (1779 Wells Branch Pkwy) posts Sunday notary hours of 11 AM–5 PM, and the Lake Creek store (9741 N Lake Creek Pkwy) posts 10:30 AM–4:30 PM. Downtown, The UPS Store Hilton Austin (500 E 4th St) is open Sundays 10 AM–4 PM. Taylor Notaries books Sunday appointments from 1 PM to 11 PM.
- **Weekday evenings:** Taylor Notaries books appointments from 6 PM to 11 PM. Among UPS Stores, Four Points (7301 Ranch Rd 620 N) is open until 7:30 PM, and Wells Branch Plaza, S Lamar Blvd and Mueller until 7 PM.
- **Saturday:** Wells Branch Plaza posts Saturday notary hours of 9 AM–5 PM; Hilton Austin and S Lamar Blvd are open 10 AM–5 PM. The Central Library takes Saturday notary appointments (10 AM–12 PM and 1–4:30 PM).
- **Late at night or at home, a hotel or a hospital:** Book a mobile notary (1MobileNotary says it books evening, weekend and holiday appointments in Austin; Taylor Notaries also says it comes to you), or use an online notary (below).
- **Cheapest:** The Central Library charges $10 for one or two signatures per document, by appointment, for simple acknowledgments and oaths.

FedEx Office's Austin location pages point notary customers to FedEx Office Online Notary with Notarize, which is done online on your own device, rather than listing an in-store notary. Call the store if you need an in-person notary. Source: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/austin/office-0115> (all Austin FedEx locations: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/austin>)

## When nothing is open: online notarization

Texas commissions online notaries: the Texas Secretary of State describes an Online Notary Public as a notary it commissions who has the same authority as a traditional notary and can perform notarizations over secure two-way audio-video (<https://www.sos.texas.gov/statdoc/notary-public.shtml>). If you book a video session with a local notary, ask whether they hold a Texas online notary commission. The national services below work by video call on your own device and generally use notaries commissioned in other states. Many documents are accepted that way, but some offices, courts, and foreign embassies may not accept an online notarization. Check with whoever will receive your document first.

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Find or check a Texas notary

The Texas Secretary of State's free Notary Search lets you look up a notary's commission by name, notary ID, ZIP code or county (<https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/notaries/notarysearch.asp>), and the state publishes its list of commissioned notaries on the Texas Open Data Portal (<https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Notary-Public-Commissions/gmd3-bnrd>). If you have an account at a bank or credit union, ask whether a notary is on staff; branch notaries usually work weekday hours only, and some branches open on Saturday mornings.
