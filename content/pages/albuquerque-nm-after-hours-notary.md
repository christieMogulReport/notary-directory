Need something notarized in Albuquerque after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are Albuquerque places that post notary hours on evenings and weekends, plus mobile notaries who come to your home, office or hospital room.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** The UPS Store's own pages say notaries are scheduled based on staff availability and recommend calling ahead or making an appointment. Holiday hours change too: all 11 stores below list Thanksgiving, November 26, as closed (November 27 is listed as regular hours), and Christmas Eve, December 24, as a short day ending at 3 PM. Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 23, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** 9 of the 11 Albuquerque UPS Stores listed below post Sunday notary hours, all 10 AM–3 PM: Ellison Dr NW (3705 Ellison Dr NW), 5901-J Wyoming Blvd NE, Menaul Blvd NE (8226 Menaul Blvd NE), Montgomery Blvd NE (11024 Montgomery Blvd NE), Central Ave SE (13170 Central Ave SE), Menaul Blvd NW (300 Menaul Blvd NW), 8100 Wyoming Blvd NE, 933 San Mateo Blvd NE and Academy Rd NE (12231 Academy Rd NE).
- **Weekday evenings:** Three stores post weekday notary hours until 6:30 PM: Ellison Dr NW, 5901-J Wyoming Blvd NE and Menaul Blvd NE. Five more post notary hours until 6 PM: Montgomery Blvd NE, Central Ave SE, Menaul Blvd NW, 8100 Wyoming Blvd NE and 933 San Mateo Blvd NE.
- **Saturday:** All 11 stores below post Saturday notary hours. Most run 9 AM–5 PM; 3167 San Mateo NE opens earliest, at 8 AM.
- **After 6:30 PM, after 3 PM on Sunday, or at home or a hospital:** The three mobile notaries below say they take evening and weekend appointments. High Desert Mobile Notary says it also books at night.

A UPS Store's notary hours can be shorter than its store hours, so each listing shows the notary hours the store posts along with the store hours. Two stores (3167 San Mateo NE and Coors Rd NW) post no Sunday hours at all.

## When nothing is open: online notarization

New Mexico's Revised Uniform Law on Notarial Acts (RULONA, Sections 14-14A-1 to 14-14A-32 NMSA 1978) lets notaries approved by the Secretary of State as Remote Online Notaries notarize for you over audio-video technology; the notary must be physically in New Mexico during the session (<https://www.sos.nm.gov/notary-and-apostille/notary-commissions/remote-online-notarization-faqs/>). The national services below work by video call on your own device and generally use notaries commissioned in other states. Many documents are accepted that way, but some offices, courts, and foreign embassies may not accept an online notarization. Check with whoever will receive your document first.

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>

## Find or check a New Mexico notary

The New Mexico Secretary of State's notary search lets you look up notarial officers by name, commission number, city, or special services offered, including approved Remote Online Notaries (<https://enterprise.sos.nm.gov/search/notary>). The Secretary of State's office in Santa Fe is open 8 AM–5 PM, Monday through Friday (<https://www.sos.nm.gov/notary-and-apostille/notary-commissions/notary-search/>). If you have an account at a bank or credit union, ask whether a notary is on staff; branch notaries usually work weekday hours only.
