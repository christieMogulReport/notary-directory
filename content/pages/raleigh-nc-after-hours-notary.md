Need something notarized in Raleigh after work, on a Saturday, or on a Sunday? Below are Raleigh places that post notary hours on evenings and weekends, plus mobile notaries who come to your home, office or hospital room.

**Call ahead to confirm hours and notary availability.** The UPS Store's own pages say notaries are scheduled based on staff availability and recommend calling ahead or making an appointment. Holiday hours change too (all 20 stores below list Thanksgiving, November 26, as closed). Hours below were copied from each business's own website on November 21, 2026. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and don't sign the document until you are in front of the notary.

## Quick picks

- **Sunday:** 16 of the 20 Raleigh UPS Stores listed below post Sunday notary hours. The longest is The UPS Store The Harvest Plaza (9650 Strickland Rd, 10 AM–4 PM). The latest Sunday notary hours run to 5 PM at The Village District (514 Daniels St), Battle Bridge Rd (6220 Battle Bridge Rd), NC Hwy 42 W (7283 Veterans Pkwy) and Kings Grant Commons (8105 Fayetteville Rd).
- **Weekday evenings:** Twelve stores below post notary hours until 7 PM on weekdays, including North Hills (4441 Six Forks Rd), The Village District, S Wilmington St downtown and Glenwood Avenue. Triangle Notary Services posts appointment hours until 8 PM on weekdays.
- **Saturday:** All 20 stores below post Saturday notary hours. The Harvest Plaza (9 AM–6 PM) and 927 W Morgan St (12–6 PM) post the latest.
- **At home, at a hospital, or later than the stores:** The mobile notaries below advertise evening and weekend appointments. Raleigh NC Notary & Fingerprinting posts Sunday hours of 12–5 PM, and Triangle Notary Services posts Saturday and Sunday hours of 10 AM–5 PM by appointment.

A UPS Store's notary hours can be shorter than its store hours. Each listing shows the notary hours the store posts. One Raleigh UPS Store page was left out: 3901 Capital Blvd is marked "Coming Soon."

## FedEx Office in Raleigh

FedEx Office's Raleigh location pages say documents are notarized online on your own device through FedEx Online Notary with Notarize. The store can help you scan and print before and after the session. Source: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/nc/raleigh/office-1134> (all Raleigh FedEx locations: <https://local.fedex.com/en-us/nc/raleigh>)

## When nothing is open: online notarization

North Carolina's own remote electronic notarization program is still being set up. The NC Secretary of State's eNotary page says new remote platforms must meet updated standards before they are approved, and its site notes that Emergency Video Notarizations have been extended to July 1, 2027, or until the first remote electronic notarization license is issued (NC Secretary of State eNotary page: <https://www.sosnc.gov/divisions/notary/e_notary_solution_providers>). The national services below work by video call on your own device and use notaries commissioned in other states. Many documents are accepted that way, but some offices, courts, and county registers of deeds may not accept an online notarization. Check with whoever will receive your document first.

- Proof (formerly Notarize): <https://www.proof.com/>
- BlueNotary: <https://bluenotary.us/>
- OneNotary: <https://onenotary.com/>
- NotaryLive: <https://notarylive.com/>
